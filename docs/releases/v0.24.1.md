A failed AI reply could leave the panel loading when the error was one the app might have retried. The request now finishes and the error shows.

## AI

- **A failed reply ends.** Rate limits, network drops, and provider errors clear the spinner and show the message. Sending again starts a new request.

## Notes

- **Archive and restore name the conflict.** Archiving a note that is already archived, or restoring one that is not, reports that conflict.

## Sync

- **A bad notebook change fails the sync.** Malformed change data is reported as an error.
