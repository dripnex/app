A failed AI reply could leave the panel loading, or throw away the answer when the app tried again. The panel stays on that request while a retry is in progress. When the request is finished, it shows the reply or the error.

## AI

- **A retry stays on the same request.** A rate limit, network drop, or provider error that will be tried again does not clear the panel. The reply that comes back is shown.
- **A finished failure ends.** When the retries are done, the error shows and the spinner stops.

## Notes

- **Archive and restore name the conflict.** Archiving a note that is already archived, or restoring one that is not, reports that conflict.

## Sync

- **A bad notebook change fails the sync.** Malformed change data is reported as an error.
